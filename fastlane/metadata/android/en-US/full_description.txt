BitNow is an adults-only, proximity-first dating app built on encrypted peer-to-peer BitChat transport.

Create a local profile, stay hidden by default, then choose a 30-minute, 1-hour, 2-hour or 4-hour window when you want to be discoverable nearby. BitNow uses Bluetooth mesh discovery and coarse signal bands instead of pretending BLE can provide exact distance.

When both people are actively available on BitNow, profiles and dating signals are exchanged through authenticated encrypted one-to-one messages. Interest signals expire after 45 minutes. Mutual signals create a match and you can move directly into encrypted private chat.

Features:
• 18+ local profile and adult confirmation
• no phone number or central dating account
• hidden-by-default visibility
• timed nearby availability
• age, identity and intent discovery filters
• coarse proximity labels, not a map pin
• encrypted profile exchange
• 45-minute interest signals
• mutual matches
• block and report-and-block controls
• privacy reset for local dating data
• encrypted direct messaging
• offline Bluetooth mesh operation
• optional BitChat/Nostr transport features retained from upstream

BitNow does not put your profile, dating preferences or precise location in the public Bluetooth announcement. The public capability bit only indicates active BitNow availability during the visibility window.
